Visitor Guidelines

Plan Your Visit.

Five thoughtful steps to help you prepare for an unhurried, illuminating consultation with our archival furniture collection.

01

Request Your Preferred Window

Select a 60- or 90-minute appointment slot. We ensure no overlapping bookings, allowing you to linger, measure, and experience each piece without distraction.

02

Share Your Space & Proportions

If you are furnishing a specific room, feel free to bring floor plans, ceiling heights, or textile samples. We will stage lighting and proximity to reflect your architectural context.

03

Inspect Joinery & Construction

We actively encourage tactile examination. Pull drawers to inspect hand-cut dovetails, feel wax finishes, examine underside maker stamps, and test structural stability.

04

Consult Our Conservators

Speak directly with the artisans who condition each piece. Learn about reversible wax protection, environmental humidity requirements, and long-term care regimes.

05

Coordinate White-Glove Logistics

Following your selection, we arrange specialized blanket-wrapped transport, doorway clearance verification, and insured room-of-choice installation.

Have a Specific Question?

If your question is not addressed here, we invite you to write to our curatorial team directly.
