The Details Are the Story

Look Closer.

True antique furniture is never defined merely by age, but by the tangible hallmarks of artisan human touch:

Natural Patina

Gentle oxidation and hand-rubbed wax finishes developed over decades of living.

Quarter-Sawn Grain

Old-growth timber with dense ring structure and deep natural luster.

Hand-Forged Brass

Cast metal escutcheons, handles, and hinges showing honest tool marks.

Traditional Joinery

Hand-cut dovetails, mortise-and-tenon joints engineered for generations.

Furniture with a Story

The Marks of Time Are Part of the Beauty.

“Every scratch, softened edge, joint and change in tone can tell part of an object's history.”

We celebrate the quiet narratives held within seasoned timber. An antique does not conceal its passage through time; its honesty, softened angles, and hand-cut construction carry a presence no modern mass manufacture can replicate.
