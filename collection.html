Collection Philosophy

Look closer. Choose slowly.

A good piece does not always announce itself immediately. It reveals its character in the turn of a leg, the wear on an armrest, or the quiet balance of its drawers.

We encourage you to take time: compare proportions against your room dimensions, examine timber grain under natural light, and ask us any questions regarding historical provenance or conservation history.
