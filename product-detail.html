Georgian Mahogany Writing Table

A refined writing table with a balanced silhouette, cockbeaded twin frieze drawers, and naturally aged timber with original brass drop handles.

Period & Origin Georgian (1714 – 1837) · England
Primary Material Solid Cuban Mahogany, Hand-Cast Brass
Dimensions Height: 76cm · Width: 122cm · Depth: 64cm
Spatial Scale Medium (Study / Library / Salon)
Conservation Conserved in-house. Original patina and joinery preserved. Traditional beeswax luster.
